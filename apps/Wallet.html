Wallet

A personal expense tracker for people who use more than one currency and more than one account — without turning budgeting into a chore.

About

Wallet keeps track of money across multiple accounts and currencies in one place, with support for recurring income — the kind of thing most budgeting apps either overcomplicate or skip entirely.

What's inside

Multi-wallet

Track cash, bank accounts, and savings separately without losing the full picture.

Multi-currency

Mix currencies across wallets without doing the conversion math yourself.

Recurring income

Set up income that repeats on schedule instead of logging it manually every time.

Status

Core tracking and multi-wallet support are built. Still in active development — this page will be updated as new features land.
