ClassDeck

A student productivity app built around the things that actually eat your time: classes, deadlines, and the discipline to sit down and focus.

ReactPWAAndroid · Compose
About

ClassDeck started as an answer to a simple problem — school apps are either bloated with features nobody uses, or so bare they don't help at all. It's built around four things: your class schedule, your deadlines, a focus mode for when you need to actually get work done, and a pomodoro timer that doesn't look like it was designed in 2012.

What's inside

Classes

A clean weekly view of your schedule, built to be scanned in a few seconds between periods.

Deadlines

Every assignment and due date in one place, sorted by what actually matters this week.

Focus

A distraction-free mode for sitting down and getting through a task without the noise.

Pomodoro

A timer built into the same visual language as the rest of the app — no separate tool needed.

Status

The React/PWA version is built and usable. An Android version using Jetpack Compose is currently in progress, under the package com.classdeck.tudustechsystems.
